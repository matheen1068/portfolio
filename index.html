07 / Mindset

How I think about systems.

01

Reliability is a feature

Users never see your architecture. They only see whether it works when it matters, so I design for the worst night, not the average day.

02

Automate the toil

If a human does it three times, a machine should do it forever. That's why I build agents that take on repetitive FinOps and DevOps work.

03

Observe first, react never

Good monitoring turns incidents into non-events. The best outage is the one caught at the warning, long before it reaches a user.

04

Build in public, teach what you know

Shipping side projects and training others keeps my fundamentals sharp. Teaching is the fastest test of whether you really understand something.
